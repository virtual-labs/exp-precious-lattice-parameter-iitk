2.	Calculate the (h k l) planes for each peak using equation (1)<br>
3.	Calculate the corresponding values of a in each case. <br>
4.	Further, plot <b>F(θ) vs a<b> and perform a linear fitting of the data obtained.<br> 
5.	The line of best fit which will be obtained will be further extrapolated to <b>F(θ) = 0</b> and the y-intercept will give us the precise lattice parameter for the particular material.<br><br>
